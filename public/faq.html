Pricing and Payment
How much does dog grooming cost in Falmouth, MA?

At Super Pet Grooming Salon in East Falmouth, prices start at the following:

  • Bath and Full Groom: starting at $75
  • Gentle Grooming: starting at $85
  • Puppy Introduction: starting at $85
  • Nail Trim and Paw Care: starting at $20
  • De-Shedding Treatment: starting at $30
  • Spa and Hydration Treatment: starting at $50

Final pricing may vary based on the pet's breed, coat condition, and size. A $25 deposit is required at online booking to confirm the appointment.

What add-on services are available and how much do they cost?

Super Pet offers the following add-ons to any grooming appointment:

  • Dematting: $1 per minute
  • Teeth brushing: $12
  • Special shampoo: $10
  • Special conditioner: $10
  • Deep hydration treatment: priced by evaluation
What payment methods does Super Pet accept?

Super Pet accepts all major credit cards and debit cards. A $25 deposit is required at the time of online booking to secure your appointment. The remaining balance is collected at the salon on the day of the appointment.

Is there a late pickup fee?

Yes. A late pickup fee of $15 per 30 minutes applies if a pet is not picked up within the scheduled window. Please let us know in advance if you expect a delay so we can accommodate your pet comfortably.

Booking and Appointments
How do I book a grooming appointment at Super Pet?

You can book an appointment in three ways:

  1. Online: Use the booking form at superpethome.com. A $25 deposit is required to confirm.
  2. By phone: Call (508) 524-3489 during business hours, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
  3. Via Instagram: Send us a message at @superpetfalmouth.
How long does a grooming appointment take?

Appointment duration depends on the service, your pet's breed, coat condition, and size. As a general guide:

  • Nail trim only: 15 to 30 minutes
  • Bath and groom (small breed): approximately 1.5 hours
  • Bath and groom (medium breed): approximately 2 hours
  • Full groom (large or double-coated breed): up to 3 hours

Our team will give you an estimated pickup time at drop-off and will notify you when your pet is ready.

Can I stay with my pet during the grooming appointment?

We kindly ask owners to drop off their pet and return for pickup. Pets tend to stay much calmer when their owner is not nearby, as they will try to reach you instead of cooperating with the groomer. Our team will contact you as soon as your pet is ready for pickup.

Do you have a monthly grooming plan?

Yes. Super Pet offers a monthly grooming plan designed to help pets gradually become comfortable with the grooming process. This plan is especially beneficial for anxious dogs or puppies who need repeated positive experiences to build trust. Ask us about it when you book your first appointment.

Services and What to Expect
What is included in a full grooming service at Super Pet?

A full grooming service at Super Pet includes:

  • Professional bath with shampoo and conditioner
  • Hand blow dry (no cage dryers ever used)
  • Full brush-out
  • Haircut to your specifications and breed standard
  • Nail trim
  • Ear cleaning
  • Finishing touches, including a bandana

Additional services like teeth brushing, dematting, or special shampoos are available as add-ons.

Does Super Pet groom cats?

Yes. Super Pet offers professional cat grooming in a calm, dedicated space that is completely separate from the dog grooming area. Our cat grooming team is trained specifically in feline handling, coat care, and breed-specific styling for all cat breeds.

Do you groom anxious or fearful dogs?

Absolutely. This is one of our specialties. Our Gentle Grooming service (starting at $85) is designed specifically for anxious, nervous, or sensitive pets. We use calming techniques, positive reinforcement, and an unhurried pace so that every appointment is a positive experience. We also recommend our monthly plan for dogs who need time to acclimate.

Do you offer puppy grooming for first-time visits?

Yes. Our Puppy Introduction service (starting at $85) is a gentle, fun first grooming experience designed to help puppies build a positive association with the salon environment. We take it slow, use lots of encouragement, and make sure the session ends on a happy note so future visits are easy.

Do you use cage dryers?

Never. Super Pet does not use cage dryers at any point. Every pet is hand-dried by a groomer in a warm, safe space. This eliminates the risk of overheating, reduces stress significantly, and allows our team to monitor your pet throughout the entire drying process.

What dog breeds do you groom?

We welcome all breeds and sizes, including large and mixed breeds. Some of the breeds we groom regularly include Goldendoodles, Labradoodles, Standard and Toy Poodles, Bernese Mountain Dogs, Huskies, Shih Tzus, Maltese, Yorkshire Terriers, Corgis, German Shepherds, Bichon Frise, Cocker Spaniels, and many more. Our groomers know the correct cut and coat care for every breed.

Requirements and Policies
What vaccinations are required for a grooming appointment?

All pets must have current vaccinations before any grooming appointment. This is required to protect all animals in our care.

  • Dogs: Rabies, DHPP (distemper, hepatitis, parvovirus, parainfluenza), and Bordetella (kennel cough)
  • Cats: Rabies and FVRCP

Please bring printed or digital vaccination records to your first appointment. We may request updated records at subsequent visits if records have expired.

What happens if my dog has matting?

Dematting is available as an add-on service at $1 per minute. In cases of severe matting where dematting would cause pain or distress to your pet, we may recommend a shorter cut to remove the mats safely. Our groomers will always communicate with you before making any significant decisions about your pet's coat.

Do you accept walk-ins?

We recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred date and time, especially during peak seasons on Cape Cod. Walk-ins may be accommodated based on availability. Please call us at (508) 524-3489 before coming in to check current availability.

Location and Hours

We are a short drive from Falmouth center, Mashpee, and Sandwich, making us one of the most conveniently located grooming salons on the Upper Cape.

What are Super Pet's business hours?

Super Pet is open Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We are closed on Saturday and Sunday. Please plan your pickup before 5:00 PM to avoid late pickup fees.

Which areas of Cape Cod does Super Pet serve?

Super Pet primarily serves pet owners from East Falmouth, Falmouth, Mashpee, Sandwich, Bourne, and Barnstable. We also welcome visitors and seasonal residents from across Cape Cod and Massachusetts who are in the area and need grooming for their pet.
