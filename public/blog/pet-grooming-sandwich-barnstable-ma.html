What Do Sandwich and Barnstable Pet Owners Look for in a Groomer?

The most common reasons pet owners cite for making the trip from Sandwich or Barnstable to Super Pet Grooming Salon fall into three categories. First, anxious dogs. Many dogs that become stressed or reactive at other salons do significantly better in an environment without cage dryers. Super Pet does not use cage dryers at any point during the grooming process. Every dog is hand-dried with a blow dryer by the groomer, reducing ambient noise and allowing the groomer to monitor the dog's comfort throughout.

Second, large breeds. Not every salon on Cape Cod is equipped or comfortable working with Bernese Mountain Dogs, Great Danes, Golden Retrievers, and similar large or giant breeds. Super Pet Grooming Salon accepts all breeds and sizes and has the equipment to groom them safely and thoroughly. Third, senior and special-needs dogs. Super Pet's Gentle Grooming service ($85) is specifically designed to minimize physical and emotional stress for elderly dogs, dogs recovering from surgery, or dogs with chronic health conditions that make a standard groom too taxing.
