About Super Pet Grooming Salon

A team of passionate, certified groomers dedicated to making every pet look and feel their best in a calm, loving environment.

More Than a Grooming Salon. We Are Family.

Super Pet Grooming Salon opened its doors in East Falmouth, MA with one clear mission: provide the highest quality, most caring grooming experience on Cape Cod. Since the beginning, we have treated every dog and cat that walks through our doors as if they were our own.

Located at 122 Teaticket Hwy on Route 28, we serve the Falmouth community and all surrounding Cape Cod towns. Our salon was built on the belief that grooming should never be stressful for your pet. We use calming techniques, positive reinforcement, and a one-on-one approach so every animal feels safe and comfortable throughout the entire visit.

We are proud to be Google Guaranteed and to hold a perfect 5-star rating based on dozens of verified Google reviews from pet owners across Cape Cod. Our team has groomed over 500 happy pets and counting.

What Sets Us Apart

We do grooming differently. Here is what our clients notice from the very first visit.

🐾

One-on-One Grooming

Every pet gets dedicated, individual attention throughout their entire appointment. No group sessions, no rushing, no distractions. Your pet is our only focus while they are with us.

🛁

No Cage Drying

We never use cage dryers. After each bath, your pet is hand-dried in a warm, comfortable space. This keeps stress levels low and allows us to monitor each animal closely.

💛

Gentle Handling

We use positive reinforcement and calming techniques for every pet, especially those who are anxious or have had difficult grooming experiences in the past. Patience is our specialty.

✂️

Breed-Specific Knowledge

Our groomers know the correct coat care and grooming styles for all breeds, from Goldendoodles and Bernese Mountain Dogs to Persians and Maine Coons. No guessing, ever.

🏅

Google Guaranteed

Super Pet is Google Guaranteed, which means our business, team, and quality standards have been independently verified. You can book with complete confidence.

📍

Locally Rooted

We are a locally owned business, not a franchise. Every dollar you spend supports a small team of Cape Cod pet lovers who genuinely care about your animal and this community.

Our Certifications and Standards

We hold ourselves to the highest standards so you never have to wonder if your pet is in good hands.
