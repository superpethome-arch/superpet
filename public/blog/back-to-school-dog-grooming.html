Back to School Season: The Best Time to Groom Your Dog Before Summer Ends

August 6, 2026 · 5 min read · Super Pet Grooming Salon

As August winds down and the back-to-school season begins, Cape Cod dog owners have one last window to book a professional summer groom before the fall schedule takes over. At Super Pet Grooming Salon in East Falmouth, August is the busiest month of the year, and spots fill up fast. If your dog has spent the summer swimming, running on the beach, and soaking up the Cape Cod sun, now is the time to get them refreshed, de-shed, and ready for the cooler months ahead.

Why August Is the Best Month to Groom Before Fall

August marks the peak of summer coat buildup. By now, most dogs have accumulated weeks of salt water exposure, sand, sunlight, and outdoor activity in their fur. The coat that kept them warm in spring has grown long and heavy, and the undercoat that will naturally start shedding in September is already loosening.

A professional groom in August accomplishes several things at once. It removes salt, sand, and residue that regular home bathing cannot fully address. It trims and shapes the coat to a comfortable length heading into fall activities. And if you add a de-shedding treatment, it removes the loose undercoat before it ends up all over your floors and furniture when the seasonal transition begins.

Waiting until September or October means the shed has already happened inside your home. Grooming in August means you stay ahead of it.

What a Full Groom in August Includes at Super Pet

The Bath and Full Groom at Super Pet Grooming Salon (from $75) is the most popular August service. It includes everything your dog needs for a complete summer refresh:

  • Full bath with professional-grade shampoo appropriate for your dog's coat type. This addresses salt buildup, beach residue, and general summer grime that home baths leave behind.
  • Blow dry and brush-out. Every dog is hand-dried by Sabina. No cage drying is used at any point. The blow-out also loosens additional dead coat and prepares it for trimming.
  • Breed-appropriate haircut. Whether your dog needs a full trim or a light tidy, the cut is tailored to the breed standard and your preferences.
  • Nail trim. Included in the full groom service, keeping nails at a healthy length heading into fall walks on pavement and harder terrain.
  • Ear cleaning. Summer ear infections are common in dogs that swim regularly. A professional ear clean at grooming time helps reduce the risk.
  • Bandana or bow finish. Every dog leaves Super Pet looking their best.

Add-ons available for August appointments include de-shedding ($30), spa treatment ($50), teeth brushing ($12), and dematting at $1 per minute for dogs with significant tangles.

The Back-to-School Window Is Short

When the kids go back to school, the summer rhythm changes fast. Schedules get tighter, weekend trips to the beach stop, and the casual Cape Cod pace gives way to fall routines. September also brings the first rain and mud season on Cape Cod. Dogs that were damp with ocean water in August are now damp with rain and mud, and a coat that was not properly groomed before fall becomes harder to manage.

Booking a groom in August means getting it done while the weather is still cooperative, the dog's coat is at its summer peak, and your own schedule has a little more flexibility. The window between now and the end of August is genuinely short. Late August slots at Super Pet Grooming Salon fill up as fast as any month of the year.

De-Shedding Before Fall: Why It Matters

De-shedding is one of the most requested add-ons at Super Pet Grooming Salon in August, and for good reason. Double-coated breeds like Labrador Retrievers, Golden Retrievers, Huskies, and Bernese Mountain Dogs build up significant undercoat through the summer. As fall approaches, that undercoat loosens and starts falling out naturally. Without a professional de-shed, most of it ends up on your floors, furniture, and clothing throughout September and October.

A professional de-shedding treatment at Super Pet removes the loose undercoat mechanically before it sheds. The result is a significant reduction in the amount of hair that falls inside your home over the following weeks. The treatment takes extra time and uses specialized tools, but at $30 as an add-on to any groom, it is one of the highest-value services available for heavy-shedding breeds.

If your dog is a double-coated breed that has been swimming and running outside all summer, adding de-shedding to your August groom is a practical investment. Learn more in our guide on de-shedding treatment for dogs.

De-shedding is a professional treatment that removes loose undercoat hair before your dog sheds it naturally indoors.
