What to Know Before Your First Drive from Bourne

Booking your appointment is simple. Call Super Pet Grooming Salon at (508) 524-3489 or book online at superpethome.com. The salon is open Tuesday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM, and Saturday 9 AM to 1 PM, and is closed on weekends. A $25 deposit is required to confirm the appointment, and that deposit is applied to the total cost of the groom.

Most grooming appointments take between 1.5 and 3 hours depending on the breed, coat condition, and services selected. You do not need to wait at the salon. Clients from Bourne typically drop off their pet in the morning, explore East Falmouth or Falmouth, and return when their groomer calls to let them know the dog is ready. Please note that a late pickup fee of $15 per 30 minutes applies after the agreed pickup time, so plan your return accordingly.

Bring current vaccination records to your first appointment. Dogs must be current on Rabies, DHPP (the distemper combination vaccine), and Bordetella.
