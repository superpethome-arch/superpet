How Long Does Dog Grooming Take? A Complete Timeline from Drop-Off to Pick-Up

July 25, 2026 · 6 min read · Super Pet Grooming Salon

At Super Pet Grooming Salon in East Falmouth, MA, most dog grooming appointments take between 1.5 and 3 hours. The exact time depends on your dog's breed, coat condition, size, and the service selected. A simple nail trim takes 20 to 30 minutes. A full groom for a large, long-coated breed can run 3 hours or longer. Knowing what to expect before you arrive helps you plan your day with confidence.

What Is the Average Dog Grooming Time?

The table below gives a practical breakdown of grooming times by service type and coat condition at Super Pet Grooming Salon. All times are approximate and can vary based on the individual dog's behavior and coat health.

What Factors Affect How Long Dog Grooming Takes?

Several variables influence the total time from drop-off to pick-up call. Understanding them helps you set realistic expectations and plan accordingly.

  • Breed and coat type. Breeds with long, curly, or dense double coats require more time to wash, dry, and shape. Goldendoodles and Bernese Mountain Dogs are among the longest appointments at Super Pet. Short-coated breeds like Beagles and Boxers move through much faster.
  • Coat condition. A dog that has been brushed regularly at home is significantly faster to groom than one arriving with mats. Matted coats require dematting before the actual groom can begin, which adds both time and cost at $1 per minute.
  • Dog's behavior. An anxious or reactive dog needs more breaks, more reassurance, and a slower, gentler pace. Sabina, Super Pet's lead certified groomer, adjusts her approach to keep each dog comfortable, which can extend the appointment time for nervous pets.
  • Service selected. A nail trim is a quick visit. A full groom with de-shedding, spa treatment, and teeth brushing is a comprehensive appointment that covers far more ground.
  • First-time visits. Dogs being groomed professionally for the first time may need extra acclimation time. The Puppy Introduction service is specifically designed to be shorter and more positive for young dogs.

How Does Super Pet's One-on-One Grooming Affect Time?

Super Pet Grooming Salon operates on a no cage drying policy. Every dog is hand-dried and attended to individually throughout the entire appointment. This takes longer than cage drying, and that is entirely intentional.

At assembly-line grooming salons, dogs are bathed in batches, placed in a cage dryer to free up the groomer, and worked on in stages between other dogs. The result is a faster clock time but a significantly more stressful experience for the pet and a lower quality finish.

At Super Pet, your dog is the only dog being worked on during that time. Sabina moves methodically through every step, including bath, blow-dry, haircut, nail trim, ear cleaning, and finishing, without splitting attention between multiple pets. The appointment takes longer, but your dog is calmer, better cared for, and the results are noticeably more consistent. It is the reason Super Pet carries a 5.0-star Google rating with 47 or more verified reviews and has served more than 500 pets across Cape Cod.

When Will I Hear That My Dog Is Ready?

Super Pet will call or text you when your dog's groom is complete. There is no set pick-up time you need to hover around. The typical approach is to drop off at your scheduled time, go run errands or have lunch nearby, and wait for the call. Falmouth center is just five minutes away, and the Teaticket area on Route 28 has several shops and restaurants to keep you occupied.

One important note: avoid arriving back at the salon too early. Dogs can sense when their owner is nearby, and that awareness can actually make them harder to work with in the final stages of the groom. Trust the process and wait for the call.

If you need to leave for an extended period, be aware that Super Pet charges a late pickup fee of $15 per 30 minutes after the scheduled pickup time. This keeps the schedule fair for all clients with appointments that day.

How to Plan Your Day Around a Dog Grooming Appointment

For a morning drop-off, plan on a two to three hour window before the pick-up call arrives. That is enough time for a trip to the grocery store, a working lunch from a Falmouth cafe, or a few remote hours from anywhere with Wi-Fi. The wait goes quickly once you stop watching the clock.

A practical tip for summer visitors: July and August are the busiest months at Super Pet. The schedule fills up one to two weeks in advance during peak season. If you are planning a visit to Cape Cod and want to book a groom while you are here, book your slot before you arrive rather than calling the week of your trip. Does Breed Affect Grooming Time at Super Pet?

Yes, significantly. Here is how the most common breeds break down by expected appointment length at Super Pet Grooming Salon:

  • Goldendoodles, Bernese Mountain Dogs, Huskies: 2.5 to 3+ hours. These are among the most time-intensive breeds in the salon due to coat density, coat length, and the thoroughness of the blow-dry process.
  • Beagles, Labradors, and other short-coat breeds: 1 to 1.5 hours. Short, dense coats dry faster and require less scissor work, making these appointments considerably quicker.
  • Cats: typically 1.5 to 2 hours, depending on coat length and temperament.
  • Puppies (Puppy Introduction service): 45 to 60 minutes. The Puppy Introduction is intentionally shorter, focused on making the first grooming experience calm and positive rather than comprehensive. See our full guide on a puppy's first grooming appointment for what this service covers.

If you are unsure where your dog falls, call Super Pet at (508) 524-3489 and describe your dog's breed, size, and coat condition. The team will give you a realistic time estimate before you book.
