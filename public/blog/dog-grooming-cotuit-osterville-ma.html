How Far Is Super Pet from Cotuit and Osterville?

Both Cotuit and Osterville are Barnstable village communities on the south shore of Cape Cod, and Route 28 connects them directly to East Falmouth. The drive is straightforward with no highway exchanges or complicated turns.

  • Osterville to Super Pet: approximately 12 miles via Route 28 West, about 15 minutes under normal traffic conditions.
  • Cotuit to Super Pet: approximately 14 miles via Route 28 West, about 17 to 20 minutes depending on the route you take.

Directions from Osterville: Take Route 28 West toward Falmouth. Continue through Mashpee. Super Pet Grooming Salon is at 122 Teaticket Hwy in East Falmouth, on the right side of the road.

Directions from Cotuit: Take Cotuit Road south to Route 28 West, then follow Route 28 toward East Falmouth. Super Pet Grooming Salon is at 122 Teaticket Hwy on the right.

Summer traffic note: Route 28 can be slower during July and August peak hours. If you are traveling during mid-morning or late-afternoon on a weekday in summer, add 5 to 10 minutes to your estimated travel time. Booking your appointment for early in the day helps avoid the heaviest traffic windows.

The Breeds Super Pet Sees from Barnstable Villages

Cotuit and Osterville have a notably high concentration of premium and working dog breeds. Families in these communities tend to invest in dogs that require regular, professional coat care. Super Pet Grooming Salon sees all of them:

  • Goldendoodles and Labradoodles. These curly and wavy-coated breeds need professional trimming every 6 to 8 weeks. Without regular grooming, their coats mat quickly, especially after beach outings or rain.
  • Portuguese Water Dogs. A breed that has a strong presence on Cape Cod. Their single-layer, low-shedding coat needs consistent trimming and professional attention to maintain the breed-standard look and prevent matting.
  • Bernese Mountain Dogs. The long, thick tri-color coats on Berners require significant coat care. The De-Shedding add-on ($30) is especially recommended for this breed during the warmer months.
  • Labrador Retrievers and Golden Retrievers. Both breeds are extremely common in Barnstable County. A Bath and Full Groom combined with the De-Shedding treatment is ideal for keeping Labs and Goldens comfortable through the Cape Cod summer.

Sabina's experience with all of these breeds means your dog will receive a groom that is appropriate for their coat type and temperament, not a one-size-fits-all approach.

Booking Tips for Cotuit and Osterville Pet Owners

July and August are the busiest months at Super Pet Grooming Salon, driven by the summer population increase across Cape Cod. Cotuit and Osterville families with summer residences should plan ahead:

  • Book 1 to 2 weeks in advance during the peak summer season. Slots fill quickly and walk-ins are not accepted.
  • Book online at superpethome.com, call (508) 524-3489 Tuesday through Friday between 9 AM and 5 PM or Saturday 9 AM to 1 PM, or reach out via Instagram at @superpetfalmouth.
  • A $50 deposit is required to confirm your appointment. This deposit is applied directly to your total on the day of the groom. It is not an extra charge.
  • First visit: Bring proof of current Rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella vaccinations. This is required for all new clients.
  • Appointment duration: Plan for 1.5 to 3 hours depending on breed, coat condition, and services selected. You do not need to stay. The team will contact you when your dog is ready.
  • Late pickup fee: A $15 fee applies per every 30 minutes of late pickup after the appointment concludes. Please arrange your schedule so you can be on time for pickup.
