Dog Nail Trim FAQs

Common questions about nail trimming and paw care at Super Pet in Falmouth, MA.

How much does a dog nail trim cost in Falmouth, MA?

A standalone nail trim and paw care at Super Pet in East Falmouth starts at $20. If your dog is getting a full groom, nail trimming is already included at no extra charge. We accept all breeds and sizes.

How often should I trim my dog's nails?

Most dogs need their nails trimmed every 3 to 4 weeks. You will know it is time when you hear clicking on hard floors, see the nails touching the ground, or notice them beginning to curl. Long nails can cause discomfort, joint stress, and affect your dog's gait over time.

What if my dog is scared of nail trims?

For dogs that are anxious or fearful about nail trims, we recommend booking a full Gentle Grooming session so we have more time to use calming techniques and positive reinforcement. Please mention the anxiety level when booking. A standalone 15-minute nail trim can be more stressful for a fearful dog than a longer, gentler session.

Can I get a nail trim without a full groom?

Yes. Nail trimming is available as a standalone service starting at $20 at Super Pet. You do not need to book a full grooming appointment. We recommend booking online or calling (508) 524-3489 to check availability, especially on Saturdays when our hours are shorter.

Do you file nails after trimming?

Yes, nail filing is offered as an optional step after clipping. Filing smooths any sharp or rough edges left after cutting. This is especially helpful for dogs that tend to scratch floors, furniture, or people with their paws. Just let us know when you book or drop off.
