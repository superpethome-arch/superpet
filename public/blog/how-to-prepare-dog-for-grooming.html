How to Prepare Your Dog for a Grooming Appointment: 7 Simple Steps

July 28, 2026 · 6 min read · Super Pet Grooming Salon

To prepare your dog for a grooming appointment at Super Pet Grooming Salon in East Falmouth, MA, the most important steps are: let your dog relieve itself before drop-off, avoid a large meal right before the appointment, and bring current vaccination records (Rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella) to your first visit. No other special preparation is required. The seven steps below cover everything that helps the appointment go smoothly, from the morning of drop-off to the moment you say goodbye at the door.

Step 1: Let Your Dog Use the Bathroom Before Drop-Off

A bathroom break before arriving at the salon is the single most helpful thing you can do on grooming day. A full bladder during a bath or during handling is uncomfortable for your dog and creates extra work for the groomer. Taking five to ten minutes for a walk right before drop-off eliminates that risk.

The added bonus: a short walk burns off nervous energy. Dogs that arrive having had some physical activity tend to be calmer and more cooperative during the groom than dogs that have been confined in a car or crate since waking up. This matters most for puppies and for dogs that tend to be anxious in new environments.

Step 2: Avoid a Large Meal Right Before the Appointment

Some dogs experience nausea or stomach discomfort during grooming, particularly during the bathing process, when they are standing, being lifted, or held in positions they are not used to. A full stomach makes this worse.

The guideline is simple: a light meal two or more hours before the appointment is fine. Water is always okay and should never be withheld. Do not skip feeding entirely, especially for small breeds or puppies that can be affected by drops in blood sugar. The goal is to avoid drop-off on a just-fed, heavy stomach, not to send your dog in hungry.

Step 3: Bring Vaccination Records to Your First Visit

For your first appointment at Super Pet Grooming Salon, bring proof of three current vaccinations:

  • Rabies
  • DHPP (Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza)
  • Bordetella (kennel cough)

A screenshot from your vet's patient portal is fully accepted. A printed copy works just as well. After the first visit, Super Pet keeps your dog's records on file, so you will not need to bring them again unless vaccinations expire and are renewed.

If your dog's vaccinations are not current, reschedule the vet appointment first. Super Pet cannot accept dogs with lapsed vaccinations. Confirming vaccine status before booking is far easier than having to cancel on the day of the appointment.

Step 4: Do Not Bathe Your Dog Right Before

This surprises many first-time grooming clients. Bathing your dog the day before is fine. Bathing your dog the morning of the appointment is not recommended.

Wet or damp fur is significantly harder to clip, assess, and work with. A groomer evaluating your dog's coat needs to see and feel it in its natural state. If the coat is wet or freshly shampooed, it changes the texture and behavior of the fur in ways that affect the groom.

Super Pet includes a full professional bath as part of every grooming service. Let the salon handle it. If your dog rolled in something particularly unpleasant, a quick rinse is fine but skip the shampoo and let Super Pet do the full wash as planned.

Step 5: Try to Brush Out Surface Tangles Beforehand

This step is helpful but not required. For short-coated breeds, it does not apply. For long-coated dogs, golden retrievers, doodles, spaniels, and similar breeds, even five minutes of brushing at home before the appointment can make a meaningful difference.

Dematting at Super Pet is billed at $1 per minute. A few minutes of brushing at home that removes surface tangles can reduce the dematting time needed at the salon and make the appointment more comfortable for your dog. Focus on the areas that mat fastest: behind the ears, under the collar, in the armpits, and at the base of the tail.

If your dog resists brushing or becomes anxious when you try, stop and let Super Pet handle it. Never force a brushing session. Forcing it creates a negative association with grooming-related handling, which makes future appointments harder for everyone.

Step 6: Tell Super Pet Anything They Should Know

At booking or at drop-off, mention anything that affects how the groom should be handled. The team notes it so Sabina, the lead certified groomer, can adjust her approach accordingly.

  • Skin sensitivities or allergies: Mention specific ingredients your dog reacts to so the right shampoo can be selected.
  • Recent surgery, injury, or arthritis: Any area that cannot be touched or manipulated should be communicated clearly before the groom begins.
  • Anxiety or reactivity: If your dog is known to be nervous at the groomer, book the Gentle Grooming service (from $85), which is designed specifically for sensitive and anxious pets.
  • First adult dog at any groomer: Mention it. Sabina adjusts her pace and handling for dogs that have never been professionally groomed before.
  • Puppies under 12 months: Ask about the Puppy Introduction service (from $85), which is designed to make the first grooming experience calm and positive.

Step 7: Drop Off and Let Go

This step sounds simple but it makes a real difference. Dogs are highly attuned to their owners' emotions and behavior. When an owner lingers at drop-off, speaks anxiously to the dog, or checks in repeatedly by phone, it communicates to the dog that something is wrong. That anxiety carries into the groom and makes the appointment longer and more stressful for everyone.

The recommended approach is straightforward: hand off your dog calmly, exchange any relevant information with the team, and go about your day. You will receive a call or text when your dog is ready for pickup. Most appointments run between 1.5 and 3 hours depending on breed, coat, and service booked.

East Falmouth is a convenient place to spend that time. Falmouth center is about five minutes away along Route 28, with plenty of cafes, restaurants, and shops. The Falmouth bike path, town beach, and library are all close by as well.

What You Do NOT Need to Do

Just as helpful as knowing what to do is knowing what to skip. None of the following are necessary before a grooming appointment at Super Pet:

  • Bring shampoo, towels, or grooming tools. Super Pet provides everything. Professional-grade products are selected based on your dog's coat and skin condition.
  • Tip. Gratuity is optional and appreciated but never expected or required.
  • Pre-clip or trim anything at home. Home trimming, especially around the face or paws, can create uneven lines that are difficult to correct. Leave it to the professionals.
  • Sedate your dog. If your dog has severe anxiety around grooming, mention it at booking and book the Gentle Grooming service. If your dog's anxiety is significant enough to require medication, speak to your veterinarian first and consult with the grooming team before the appointment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bathe my dog before a grooming appointment?

No. Super Pet Grooming Salon in East Falmouth includes a full bath as part of every grooming service. You do not need to bathe your dog beforehand. If your dog is very dirty, a light rinse is fine, but avoid shampooing right before the appointment. Wet or damp fur is harder to clip and assess.

Should I feed my dog before a grooming appointment?

Avoid a large meal right before drop-off. A light meal 2 or more hours before the appointment is fine. Some dogs experience nausea or discomfort during grooming on a full stomach, particularly during bathing or when being held in unfamiliar positions.

What vaccinations do I need to bring to Super Pet?

For your first visit to Super Pet Grooming Salon, bring proof of current Rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella vaccinations. After the first visit, Super Pet keeps the records on file. A screenshot from your vet's patient portal is accepted. If vaccinations are not current, reschedule the vet appointment before booking the groom.

Can I stay with my dog during the grooming appointment?

Super Pet asks that owners drop off and return at pickup time. Dogs tend to stay calmer and cooperate better when their owners are not present during the groom. You will receive a call or text when your dog is ready. Most appointments take between 1.5 and 3 hours.
