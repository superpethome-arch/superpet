Frequently Asked Questions

Do you groom cats?
Yes, we do groom cats, with extra care and in a calm space separate from dogs. Because cat grooming requires special handling, every cat is confirmed with our lead groomer before the appointment is booked. Just reach out and we will let you know right away.
What breeds do you groom?
We welcome dogs and cats of all breeds and sizes. Some large or heavily matted coats may require additional time and cost we'll always discuss this with you before your appointment.
Do you require up-to-date vaccinations?
Yes, for the safety of all pets in our care, we require proof of current Rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella vaccinations. Please bring your records to your first appointment.
How long does a grooming appointment take?
Most appointments take between 1.5 to 3 hours depending on the service, breed, and coat condition. We'll give you an estimated pick-up time when you drop off.
Can I stay with my pet during the groom?
For the safety and comfort of your pet (and our groomers!), we ask that owners drop off and return at pick-up time. Pets tend to behave better and stay calmer when their owners aren't present.
How do I book an appointment?
You can book online through our booking form, call us directly, or send us a message on Instagram. We'll confirm your appointment as soon as possible.
What vaccinations does my dog need to be groomed?
Dogs must have current Rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella vaccinations to be groomed at Super Pet. Please bring proof of vaccination records to your first appointment. If you are unsure, check with your vet before booking.
What should I bring to my dog's grooming appointment?
For your first visit, bring proof of current Rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella vaccinations. No other items are needed. Simply drop off your pet and we will contact you when the groom is complete, usually within 1.5 to 3 hours.
