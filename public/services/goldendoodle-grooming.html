Goldendoodle Grooming FAQs

Common questions from Goldendoodle owners at Super Pet in Falmouth, MA.

How often should a Goldendoodle be groomed?

Goldendoodles should be professionally groomed every 6 to 8 weeks to prevent matting and keep their coat healthy. Goldendoodle coats - particularly wavy and curly types - mat surprisingly quickly, especially in areas like behind the ears, under the collar, in the armpits, and around the paws. Daily or every-other-day brushing at home between appointments is also essential.

What cut should I get for my Goldendoodle?

The most popular cuts for Goldendoodles are: the teddy bear cut (rounded, fluffy face with even body length), the puppy cut (shorter all over for lower maintenance), and the kennel cut (short and very practical for active dogs). We also do custom length cuts - just tell us how long you want to keep the fur. We'll discuss the style in detail when you drop off your dog.

How do I prevent my Goldendoodle's coat from matting?

Daily brushing at home with a slicker brush and a metal comb is the most important thing you can do. Pay special attention to mat-prone areas: behind the ears, under the collar, in the armpits, and around the paws and groin. After beach trips or swimming, always brush and dry the coat. Regular professional grooming every 6 to 8 weeks is also critical to preventing severe matting.

How long does Goldendoodle grooming take?

Goldendoodle grooming at Super Pet typically takes 2 to 3 hours depending on the size, coat condition, and cut. Mini Goldendoodles may be quicker, while standard-size Doodles or those with matted coats may take longer. We'll give you a time estimate when you drop off. We text you when your dog is ready so you don't have to wait around.

Do you groom mini and standard Goldendoodles?

Yes. We groom Goldendoodles of all sizes at Super Pet in East Falmouth, MA - miniature, medium, and standard. Pricing varies by size and coat condition. Please mention your Goldendoodle's size and approximate weight when booking so we can prepare appropriately.
