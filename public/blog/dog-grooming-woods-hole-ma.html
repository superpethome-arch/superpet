Summer Visitors Arriving by Ferry in Woods Hole

Woods Hole is home to the Steamship Authority ferry terminal, the main port connecting Cape Cod to Martha's Vineyard and Nantucket. Every summer, thousands of pet owners travel through Woods Hole with their dogs, arriving from or departing to the islands.

Dogs that have spent time on Martha's Vineyard or Nantucket often arrive back on Cape Cod sandy, salty, and ready for a proper refresh. Super Pet Grooming Salon is the closest professional grooming option to the Woods Hole ferry terminal. A bath and full groom or a de-shedding treatment after a ferry trip and a beach vacation is an excellent way to get your dog clean and comfortable before the drive home.

If you are arriving at Woods Hole and plan to stay on Cape Cod for a few days before heading back, booking a grooming appointment at Super Pet is easy. Call (508) 524-3489 or book online at superpethome.com. Keep in mind that August appointments fill quickly, so booking ahead is recommended.

How to Book from Woods Hole

Booking from Woods Hole is simple. You can reserve your dog's appointment in two ways:

Online: Visit superpethome.com and select your preferred service and date. The online booking system is available at any time and will confirm your appointment immediately.

By phone: Call (508) 524-3489 Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM. The team will confirm your dog's breed, size, coat condition, and the services you want. Your price is confirmed at booking.

A $25 deposit is collected to secure your appointment. This deposit is applied directly to your total on the day of grooming. It is not an extra fee. For first-time visits, bring proof of vaccination records, including Rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella. Super Pet keeps vaccination records on file after the first visit.
