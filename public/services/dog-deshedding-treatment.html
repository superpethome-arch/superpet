De-Shedding Treatment FAQs

Common questions about de-shedding treatments for dogs at Super Pet in Falmouth, MA.

What is a de-shedding treatment for dogs?

A de-shedding treatment is a professional grooming service that removes excess loose undercoat fur from double-coated dogs using specialized shampoos, a high-velocity blow-dry, and professional de-shedding tools. Unlike regular brushing at home, the combination of products and equipment loosens fur from deep in the undercoat, removing far more than a brush alone. The result is dramatically reduced shedding in your home for weeks after the appointment.

How much does a de-shedding treatment cost?

De-shedding treatment at Super Pet starts at $30 when added to a full grooming appointment. As a standalone service, pricing varies based on your dog's breed, coat density, and the time required. Call us at (508) 524-3489 for a quote for your specific dog.

How long does a de-shedding treatment take?

A de-shedding treatment typically adds 30 to 60 minutes to a standard grooming appointment. For very heavy-coated breeds like Huskies, Bernese Mountain Dogs, or Great Pyrenees, it can take longer. When combined with a full bath and groom, the entire appointment may run 2 to 3.5 hours for large double-coated breeds.

How often should I get a de-shedding treatment?

Most double-coated dogs benefit from a de-shedding treatment every 4 to 8 weeks. During heavy shedding seasons (typically spring and fall), more frequent treatments can make a significant difference in managing fur around the home. Regular treatments also improve coat health over time.

Does de-shedding hurt my dog?

No. A professional de-shedding treatment at Super Pet is a comfortable process for your dog. We use gentle specialized shampoos, a high-velocity dryer (which most dogs actually enjoy), and careful, professional brush-out tools. We never aggressively pull fur or cause discomfort. The treatment is removing fur that is already loose and ready to shed.
