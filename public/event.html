What Happens at the Event

No rigid appointment slots. Drop by, check in, and let us take care of the rest.

  1. 1

    Arrive any time between 1 PM and 3 PM

    There are no fixed appointment slots within the event window. Come when it is convenient for you and your dog.

  2. 2

    Quick check-in at the front desk

    Our team greets you, confirms your RSVP, and gets you set up. New clients do a brief registration at this point: name, dog info, and rabies confirmation.

  3. 3

    Professional nail trim by Sabina or the team

    A gentle, thorough nail trim performed by certified groomers. The service typically takes 15 to 20 minutes. No cage waiting. Individual attention for every pet.

  4. 4

    Your dog leaves with happy, trimmed paws

    That is it. Completely free, no obligation to book future services. We just love dogs and want to celebrate National Dog Day with the Cape Cod community.

Heads up: We are capping capacity to keep the experience calm and stress-free for every pet. An RSVP is the best way to secure your spot. Walk-ins are welcome but cannot be guaranteed.

Questions About the Event

Is the nail trim really free?

Yes. On National Dog Day, August 26, 2026 between 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM, Super Pet offers one complimentary professional nail trim per dog. No hidden fees, no obligation to book future services.

Do I need an appointment?

An RSVP is encouraged to help manage capacity, but walk-ins are welcome during the event hours. Arrive any time between 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM. RSVPs get priority if the event reaches capacity.

What vaccinations does my dog need?

Your dog must have a current Rabies vaccination to participate. Bring proof of vaccination (printed copy or screenshot from your vet's portal) if it is your first visit to Super Pet. Returning clients on file are all set.

Can I bring more than one dog?

Yes, each dog in your group qualifies for one free nail trim. Please mention the number of dogs when you RSVP so we can plan accordingly.

What if my dog is anxious or reactive?

Sabina, Super Pet's lead certified groomer, is experienced with nervous and reactive dogs. The nail trim is a quick, gentle service. If your dog has had difficult experiences with nail trims before, let the team know at check-in and we will work with your pet at their pace.
