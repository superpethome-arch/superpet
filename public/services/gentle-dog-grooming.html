Gentle Grooming FAQs

Answers to common questions about gentle and anxiety-friendly grooming at Super Pet.

What is gentle grooming?

Gentle grooming is a specialized, low-stress approach designed for dogs that experience anxiety, fear, or sensitivity during the grooming process. At Super Pet, it means using positive reinforcement, calming techniques, an unhurried pace, and never forcing your dog into positions or through steps they resist. We take as long as your dog needs - no exceptions.

Is gentle grooming more expensive than a regular groom?

Gentle grooming starts at $85 compared to $75 for a standard full groom. The difference reflects the additional time and care required. Some sessions may take longer than a standard appointment, and the final price may reflect that. We always discuss pricing clearly before starting.

My dog has bitten groomers before - can you help?

We understand that some dogs have had difficult or traumatic past grooming experiences. Please mention any biting history when booking so we can prepare appropriately. We assess each dog individually and will always prioritize safety for your dog and our team. In some cases, we may recommend a gradual introduction over multiple shorter visits before a full groom.

How do you calm an anxious dog during grooming?

We use a range of techniques: slow, deliberate movements; calm, reassuring voices; frequent pauses and breaks; positive reinforcement with treats and praise; minimal restraint; and continuous reading of your dog's body language. We never rush, and we adapt our approach in real time based on how your dog is responding.

How many visits before my dog gets comfortable?

Every dog is different. Some become noticeably calmer after just two or three visits. Others need more time. What helps most is consistency - monthly visits to the same groomer in the same environment allow your dog to build positive associations gradually. We recommend our monthly grooming plan for anxious dogs because it creates that consistent rhythm.
