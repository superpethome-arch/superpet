About the Event

One dog. One nail trim. Completely free.

To celebrate National Dog Day, Super Pet Grooming Salon in East Falmouth is opening its doors for two hours of complimentary professional nail trims. Certified groomer Sabina and the team take care of everything. You just show up with a happy dog.

All breeds welcome. Drop in any time between 1 and 3 PM. No fixed time slots. The only requirement: rabies vaccination up to date.

122 Teaticket Hwy, East Falmouth, MA · Route 28, easy parking on site

  1. 1

Before You Arrive

Two things to know.

💉

Rabies Vaccination Required

Your dog must have a current rabies vaccination to participate. Bring proof (printed or a screenshot from your vet's portal) if this is your first visit. Returning clients on file are all set.

🐕

Dog on a Leash Required

Keep your dog leashed upon arrival and in the waiting area. It keeps everyone safe and comfortable, especially during a busy event with multiple dogs visiting at once.

Not sure about vaccination status? Call us at (508) 524-3489 and we can help you check before you make the trip.

Common Questions

Anything we did not cover above.

Is the nail trim really free?

Yes. On National Dog Day, August 26, 2026, between 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM, Super Pet offers one complimentary professional nail trim per dog. No hidden fees, no obligation to book future services.

Do I need an appointment?

An RSVP is encouraged to help manage capacity, but walk-ins are welcome during the event hours. Arrive any time between 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM. RSVPs get priority if the event reaches capacity.

What vaccinations does my dog need?

Your dog must have a current Rabies vaccination to participate. Bring proof of vaccination if it is your first visit to Super Pet. Returning clients on file are all set.

Can I bring more than one dog?

Yes, each dog in your group qualifies for one free nail trim. Please mention the number of dogs when you RSVP so we can plan accordingly.

What if my dog is anxious or reactive?

Sabina, Super Pet's lead certified groomer, is experienced with nervous and reactive dogs. The nail trim is a quick, gentle service. If your dog has had difficult experiences before, let the team know at check-in and they will work with your pet at their pace.
