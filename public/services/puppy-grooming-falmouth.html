Puppy Grooming FAQs

Common questions from puppy parents about their dog's first grooming experience at Super Pet in Falmouth, MA.

When should I start grooming my puppy?

Most puppies are ready for their first grooming appointment between 10 and 16 weeks of age, once they have had their initial vaccinations (Rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella). Starting grooming during this early socialization window helps puppies learn that grooming is a normal, safe, and even enjoyable part of life. The earlier you start, the easier grooming will be throughout your dog's life.

What happens at a puppy introduction?

A puppy introduction at Super Pet is a short, positive session designed to familiarize your puppy with the grooming environment. It includes a gentle bath, light brush-out, nail trim, ear check, and a bandana. We go at your puppy's pace throughout, and we stop for breaks whenever your puppy needs them. There is no pressure to complete every step if your puppy isn't ready - the goal is a positive experience.

What vaccinations does my puppy need?

Puppies must have current Rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella vaccinations before their first grooming appointment at Super Pet. Most puppies complete their initial vaccine series between 10 and 16 weeks. Your vet can tell you exactly when your puppy is ready, and they can provide a copy of the vaccination records to bring to your first appointment.

How long does a puppy grooming appointment take?

Puppy introduction appointments are shorter than standard adult grooms - typically 45 minutes to 1.5 hours. We go at your puppy's pace. If they need breaks or extra reassurance, the session may take a little longer. That is completely fine and expected on early visits.

How often should puppies be groomed?

After the first introduction visit, we recommend bringing your puppy in for grooming monthly. Consistent monthly visits help your puppy build positive associations with the grooming environment, keep their coat healthy and manageable, and prevent matting as their adult coat grows in. Regular grooming from an early age sets the foundation for a lifetime of easy, stress-free appointments.
