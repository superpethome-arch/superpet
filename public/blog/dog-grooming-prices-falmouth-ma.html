Why Do Dog Grooming Prices Vary?

No two dogs are identical, and grooming prices reflect that. The main factors that affect cost at any professional salon in the Falmouth area include:

  • Breed and coat type. A short-haired Beagle takes far less time than a Bernese Mountain Dog with a dense double coat. Longer coats require more product, more drying time, and more careful scissor work.
  • Size. Larger dogs need more shampoo, longer rinse cycles, and more handling time. A Great Dane bath is simply not the same effort as a Chihuahua bath.
  • Coat condition. Matted fur requires dematting before grooming can begin. At Super Pet Grooming Salon, dematting is billed at $1 per minute rather than a flat fee, so you pay exactly for the time needed, nothing more.
  • Service selected. A standalone nail trim ($20) is a quick visit. A full groom with spa add-ons and teeth brushing is a comprehensive appointment that takes considerably longer.
