Large Dog Grooming FAQs

Common questions about large and giant breed dog grooming at Super Pet in Falmouth, MA.

Do you groom large dogs?

Yes. Super Pet in East Falmouth welcomes dogs of all sizes, including large and giant breeds. We have hands-on experience grooming Bernese Mountain Dogs, Siberian Huskies, German Shepherds, Labradors, Golden Retrievers, Standard Poodles, Rottweilers, Great Danes, and many other large breeds. All breeds are welcome at our Cape Cod salon.

How much does grooming a large dog cost?

Large dog grooming at Super Pet starts at $75 and increases based on size, breed, coat condition, and the services needed. For very large breeds or dogs requiring de-shedding or dematting, pricing will be higher. We always discuss pricing clearly before starting any work. For first-time large dogs over 70 lbs, our groomer reviews the dog before the deposit is charged.

How long does grooming a large dog take?

Grooming a large dog at Super Pet typically takes 2 to 3 hours. For giant breeds like Great Danes or Bernese Mountain Dogs, or for dogs that need de-shedding or dematting, appointments may run up to 3.5 to 4 hours. We will give you an estimated time range when you drop off and text you when your dog is ready.

What large breeds do you specialize in?

We groom all large breeds at Super Pet, including Bernese Mountain Dog, Siberian Husky, Alaskan Malamute, German Shepherd, Belgian Malinois, Labrador Retriever, Golden Retriever, Standard Poodle, Irish Setter, Rottweiler, Doberman, Great Dane, and many others. If you're unsure whether we can accommodate your dog, just call us.

Why does my large dog need a groomer evaluation on the first visit?

For first-time large dogs over 70 lbs, our groomer personally reviews the dog before confirming the appointment. This allows us to understand the dog's temperament, assess the coat condition, and plan the right approach for a safe and positive experience. We want to make sure the grooming session is right for both the dog and our team. No deposit is charged until the review is complete and the appointment is confirmed.
