What to Expect on Your First Visit from Mashpee

First-time visitors from Mashpee can expect a smooth, organized experience at Super Pet Grooming Salon. Here is what the process looks like:

Booking. Call (508) 524-3489 Monday through Friday between 9 AM and 5 PM, or book online at superpethome.com. The team will ask about your dog's breed, size, coat condition, and the services you want. Your price is confirmed at this stage. A $25 deposit is collected to secure the appointment. This deposit is applied to your total on the day of grooming. It is not an extra charge.

What to bring. For first-time visits, bringing vaccination records is recommended. The salon may ask about your dog's health history, any known sensitivities, and whether the dog has been groomed before. If your dog is particularly anxious, mentioning this at booking helps the team plan accordingly.

Appointment duration. Plan for 1.5 to 3 hours depending on the service and your dog's breed and coat. Because Super Pet Grooming Salon does not use cage drying, drying time is included in the hands-on appointment. You will receive a call or message when your dog is ready for pickup.
