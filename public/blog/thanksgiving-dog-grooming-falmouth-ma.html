Thanksgiving Dog Grooming in Falmouth, MA: Book Before the Holiday Rush

September 23, 2026 · 5 min read · Super Pet Grooming Salon

Thanksgiving 2026 is Thursday, November 26.

Holiday weeks have fewer open days. Book your dog's Thanksgiving groom early, ideally 2 to 3 weeks ahead, for Saturday, November 21, Tuesday, November 24, or Wednesday, November 25.

Book a Thanksgiving Groom

Thanksgiving on Cape Cod usually means a full house: family visiting from off-Cape, kids on the floor, and a dog who wants to greet every single person. A clean, freshly groomed dog makes the day easier for everyone, and it means less dog hair on the guest room bedding. Here is how to plan it.

When to Book Your Dog's Thanksgiving Groom

The short answer: groom 2 to 5 days before the holiday, and book 2 to 3 weeks in advance.

Grooming too early (two weeks before) means the coat is already picking up dirt again by Thursday. Grooming on the day itself is not realistic, and the salon's hours during the holiday itself can differ from the regular schedule. The sweet spot for Thanksgiving 2026 is:

  • Saturday, November 21 (9 AM to 1 PM): good for families who travel early in the week
  • Tuesday, November 24: a strong choice for most dogs
  • Wednesday, November 25: the day before, so your dog is at its freshest

Super Pet's regular hours are Tuesday to Friday 9 AM to 5 PM and Saturday 9 AM to 1 PM, closed Sunday and Monday. Please call (508) 524-3489 to confirm holiday week hours.

Which Service to Choose Before Guests Arrive

It depends on when your dog was last groomed and how much it sheds.

If it has been more than 6 weeks: book a bath and full groom (from $75). It includes a bath, hand blow-dry, brush-out, haircut or tidy, nail trim, and ear cleaning.

If your dog sheds heavily: add a de-shedding treatment (from $30). This is the single best way to reduce hair on furniture and guests' clothes during the visit.

If your dog was groomed recently: a bath with a nail trim (from $20) is often enough. Short nails are also kinder to guests' legs when your dog jumps up to say hello.

If your dog gets nervous around crowds or new places: consider our gentle grooming service (from $85), which runs at a slower pace with extra breaks. It is better to have a calm grooming day earlier in the week than a stressful one right before the house fills up.

Holiday Tips for Cape Cod Dog Owners

Keep the table food away

Cooked turkey bones, onions, garlic, grapes, raisins, chocolate, fatty skin, and heavily seasoned dishes can make dogs sick. Ask guests not to share scraps, and check with your veterinarian before offering anything from the table.

Give your dog a quiet space

Even social dogs get tired in a busy house. Set up a quiet room with water, a bed, and a chew toy where your dog can rest away from the noise.

Watch the door

With guests coming and going, doors stay open longer. Make sure your dog wears a collar with an ID tag and that the microchip information is up to date.

Brush between the groom and the holiday

A quick daily brush in the days after the groom keeps the coat looking its best until Thursday, especially for curly and long-coated breeds.
